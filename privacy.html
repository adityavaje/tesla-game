Privacy

Roadster Loop runs entirely in your browser. It does not ask for an account and does not send your gameplay to any server.

Local storage. Your best score is saved in your browser's localStorage under one key. Clear your site data to remove it. If storage is unavailable, the game still works and simply won't remember your best.

Third-party resources. The page loads three.js from cdnjs.cloudflare.com and a font from Google Fonts. Those providers may receive your IP address and browser details when you load the page, under their own policies.

Advertising. If the site owner enables ads, ad partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to show and measure ads. Update this section to name your ad provider and describe your consent options before enabling ads.
